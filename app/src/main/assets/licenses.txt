NOCTURNE — SOUNDS, SOFTWARE AND LICENCES

Instrument names such as "Yamaha-inspired", "Steinway-inspired", "Bösendorfer-inspired" and "Fazioli-inspired" describe a sound character only. Nocturne is not affiliated with, endorsed by or partnered with any piano manufacturer. All trademarks belong to their owners.

All sample libraries below were converted by Nocturne: notes were trimmed, loudness-normalised, given loop points for long sustain, re-mapped across the keyboard and encoded as compressed SoundFont 3 files. Sound character is further shaped by Nocturne's engine (filters, EQ, reverb).
